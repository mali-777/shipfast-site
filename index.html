How it works

From a list of addresses to a full day of routes in three steps.

  1. Step 1

    Import today's stops

    Upload a spreadsheet of orders or connect your order system. ShipFast checks every address before it plans.

  2. Step 2

    Set your rules

    Tell ShipFast about your vans, drivers, shift times, delivery windows and how much each vehicle can carry.

  3. Step 3

    Get routes in minutes

    ShipFast builds a short, balanced route for every driver and sends it to their phone, ready to go.
